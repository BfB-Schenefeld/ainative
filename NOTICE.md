# Notice

This repository stores excerpts of third-party training content — currently Scaled
Agile, Inc. course material — captured under personal learner access for the purpose of
study and personal knowledge management.

- Every source record carries `rights.redistribution: none`.
- Verbatim provider prose is marked `verbatim: true` and kept to what is needed to
  preserve meaning; everything else is paraphrased.
- No answer keys are inferred or invented. `answer_status: unknown` where the key was
  not captured.

**This repository is currently public.** Verbatim excerpts of licensed training material
in a public repository is a rights risk regardless of the notices above. Making it
private is a one-click change and is the recommended default for this content.
