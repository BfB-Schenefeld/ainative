Welcome

Welcome to the AI-Native SAFe Overview

AI-Native SAFe builds on a foundation of Lean-Agile practices by powering organizations with Lean, Agile, and AI. In this path, you will learn about four critical shifts and the human-centric AI culture that allows organizations using Lean-Agile practices to master the adoption of AI.

Completing this upgrade path and passing the final quiz will upgrade your current SAFe certifications to their new AI-Native SAFe versions.

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • Explain why organizations need AI-Native SAFe to achieve a return on AI
  • Explain the four shifts to become AI-Native and how each changes day-to-day practice
  • Describe the human skills the AI-Native Professional pairs with AI fluency
  • Locate your own role in AI-Native SAFe
If you hold an expert certification (SPC, ASPC, or SPCT), you must also complete Adopting AI-Native SAFe to upgrade your certifications. Adopting AI-Native SAFe is an on-demand course available in your learning plan.
