# Certifications

`SPC`, `ASPC` and `SPCT` are recorded because the Welcome lesson names them explicitly as
expert certifications with an extra upgrade requirement. The expansions of the
abbreviations are **not** in the capture and are recorded here as working labels —
verify them against a Scaled Agile source before relying on them.

Non-expert SAFe certifications are also upgradeable ("your current SAFe certifications")
but none are named in any capture yet.
