# Welcome to the AI-Native SAFe Overview

## Framing

AI-Native SAFe is positioned as building on a foundation of Lean-Agile practices,
powering organizations with Lean, Agile and AI together. The course covers **four
critical shifts** plus a **human-centric AI culture** that, per Scaled Agile, is what
lets organizations already running Lean-Agile practices master AI adoption.
[^b0033]

## Certification effect

Completing this upgrade path and passing the final quiz upgrades the learner's existing
SAFe certifications to their AI-Native SAFe versions. [^b0034]

Holders of an expert certification — **SPC, ASPC or SPCT** — must additionally complete
*Adopting AI-Native SAFe*, an on-demand course delivered through the learning plan.
[^b0034]

## Learning objectives

By the end of the course the learner can:

1. Explain why organizations need AI-Native SAFe to achieve a return on AI. [^b0036]
2. Explain the four shifts to become AI-Native and how each changes day-to-day practice. [^b0037]
3. Describe the human skills the AI-Native Professional pairs with AI fluency. [^b0038]
4. Locate their own role in AI-Native SAFe. [^b0039]
