# Optimized for outcome-driven flow

**Shift #1**

## Objectives

1. Explain the outcome-driven product development cycle adopted by AI-Native ARTs
2. Evaluate if an OKR is well-formed, and recognize how OKRs align your organization at every level
3. Recognize why AI compute costs need ongoing management instead of fixed budgeting
4. Explore the concept of curated data and judge whether a data source meets the four qualities of curated data
5. Score your organization against the critical success factors

## Structure

Facilitator outline as the companion app defines it. Activity prose and
slide content are not in the bundle, so this is structure, not teaching copy.

- **Activity** `2.1.a` Compare workflows — Slide 2-6 · Sketch: 4 min · Compare: 6 min · Share: 3 min

- **Assess** `2.1.c` SF1: Outcome-driven product development — Slides 2-11–2-13
