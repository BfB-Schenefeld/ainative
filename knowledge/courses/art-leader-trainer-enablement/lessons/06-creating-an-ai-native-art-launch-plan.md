# Creating an AI-Native ART launch plan

**Workshop**

## Objectives

1. Resolve gaps from your assessment into specific, owned actions
2. Build a launch plan that sequences these actions before PI Outcome Planning, before Sense and Respond, or after both
3. Commit to the plan as a group
4. Discuss how you will share your plan and prepare for what happens after this class
