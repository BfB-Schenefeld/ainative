An evolution of SAFe that builds on a foundation of Lean-Agile practices and powers
organizations with Lean, Agile and AI. Its content is organized around four critical
shifts plus a human-centric AI culture, which together are presented as what enables an
organization already using Lean-Agile practices to master AI adoption.

Existing SAFe certifications are upgraded to AI-Native SAFe versions by completing the
corresponding upgrade path and passing its final quiz.
